Professional Price Action Framework

The Consistent Trader.

Forget commercial indicators and get-rich-quick gimmicks. This is the structural framework used by professional traders to preserve capital, exploit systemic inefficiencies, and extract consistent profit from order flow.

Phase 01 / Risk Management

The 1% Capital Lock

Amateurs focus exclusively on how much they can win; professionals focus entirely on protecting what they have. If your account is $100, your maximum risk per trade is exactly $1.00 (1%). You do not increase position sizing until your edge is mathematically proven over a 100-trade sample size.

  • Never risk more than 1% per setup
  • Hard Stop-Loss always set in server
  • Max 3% total daily drawdown limit
Phase 02 / Structural Context

High vs Low Timeframe

Candlestick patterns are completely meaningless without structural context. You must evaluate the trend on a Higher Timeframe (HTF like H4 or H1) to identify major institutional Supply and Demand zones. Only zoom into the Lower Timeframes (LTF like M15 or M5) to hunt execution triggers.

HTF Context: Market Structure (Trend)
Key Levels: Major Support & Resistance
Execution: Patterns matching HTF order flow
Phase 03 / Trade Execution

The Inefficiency Trap

Stop chasing moving green candles. Wait patiently for institutional liquidity sweeps (fake breakouts targeting retail stop losses) or Fair Value Gaps (FVGs). Let price action pull back into your defined execution parameters, accept the trade at a premium or discount, and run the system like a machine.

  • Entry: Rejection at structural limits
  • Target: Next opposing liquidity pool
  • Minimum Risk-to-Reward ratio of 1:2
Phase 04 / Professional Review

The Quantitative Journal

Trading is an absolute game of data and statistical consistency, not emotional intuition. Log every single execution, capture clean chart screenshots of your entries and exits, and measure your exact win rate and profit factor. Treat this discipline like a high-end operating business or the market will quickly liquidate you.

Top 10 Institutional Candlestick Patterns

Note: These patterns are only valid when forming directly at key High Timeframe Support, Demand, Resistance, or Supply zones.

01 / THE HAMMER

Bullish Liquidity Rejection

Forms at the bottom of a downtrend. Shows aggressive sellers were heavily trapped and entirely overtaken by heavy buying volume before the close.

BULLISH REVERSAL
02 / INVERTED HAMMER

Buyer Accumulation Alert

Located at structural support floors. Signals that buyers are actively stepping into the market to test and challenge bear dominance.

BULLISH CONFIRMATION
03 / BULLISH ENGULFING

Institutional Order Sweep

A prominent green body completely swallows the previous red candle's trading range, showing an aggressive transfer of market control.

STRONG MOMENTUM LONG
04 / BEARISH ENGULFING

Distribution Shift

A large red body totally overwhelms the preceding green candle at premium zones, signaling institutional profit-taking and aggressive short entries.

STRONG MOMENTUM SHORT
05 / SHOOTING STAR

Premium Supply Rejection

Occurs at the top of an uptrend. Retail breakout traders are caught off guard and trapped, while institutions aggressively drive prices down into a low close.

BEARISH REVERSAL
06 / MORNING STAR

Accumulation Cycle Floor

A highly reliable 3-candle accumulation pattern showing the step-by-step exhaustion of sellers followed by a strong influx of structural buyers.

3-CANDLE BUY REVERSAL
07 / EVENING STAR

Distribution Cycle Ceiling

The exact opposite of the Morning Star. Indicates clear upward trend exhaustion, followed by an aggressive return of institutional short sellers.

3-CANDLE SELL REVERSAL
08 / THE DOJI

Volatility Compression

The opening and closing prices are completely identical. It shows total market equilibrium and contraction, which usually precedes an explosive expansion breakout.

EQUILIBRIUM / WAIT
09 / MARUBOZU

Pure Trend Imbalance

A solid, blocky candle with no wicks. It means one side dominated the asset completely from the opening bell to the final second, proving clean institutional direction.

PURE MOMENTUM DRIVE
10 / INSIDE BAR

Order Book Range Restriction

The inside candle rests completely within the high and low range of the previous candle. This flags order compression right before a strong breakout expansion.

COMPRESSION / BREAKOUT WATCH

A bullish pattern formed in the middle of nowhere is random noise. The exact same pattern formed after sweeping a major High Timeframe liquidity pool is an institutional trade setup.
