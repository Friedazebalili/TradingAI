The Precision Workflow: 1H FVG to LTF CHoCH

Stop placing limit orders blindly. Confirm the algorithmic shift on the lower timeframe.

Step 01 // Identify Context 1H Chart

The Fair Value Gap (FVG)

An FVG represents a severe algorithmic imbalance. Price moved so fast that buyers/sellers were left behind. You do not trade simply because you see an FVG. You mark the 1-Hour FVG as a Point of Interest (POI). When price returns to this zone, it is seeking liquidity to rebalance.

1H FVG (POI)
Step 02 // Confirm Shift 5M / 1M Chart

Change of Character (CHoCH)

Zoom into the 5M chart when price hits your 1H FVG. Price will naturally be in a downtrend. A CHoCH occurs when price suddenly breaks above the last lower high. This confirms that institutions are actively defending the 1H FVG and order flow has shifted bullish. Execute on the pullback.

1H FVG ZONE

Advanced Breakout Mechanics

How to distinguish between an engineered trap and genuine structural delivery.

1. The Fakeout (Sweep Trap)

Price breaks a key high, triggering retail breakout buyers, only to instantly reverse and trap them.

BSL (Buy Side Liquidity)
TRAP
Action: Look for a Shooting Star or Bearish Engulfing immediately after the high is taken. Short the rejection.

2. The CHoCH Breakout

Price taps a HTF FVG, compresses, and violently breaks structural highs, confirming intent.

Old Lower High
CHoCH
Action: This is your green light. The breakout is real because it is backed by HTF liquidity from the FVG.

3. The Mitigation Retest

A clean structural break leaves an order block behind. Price returns later to mitigate (retest) and goes.

BOS (Break of Structure)
RETEST
Action: Wait for the retest into the blue order block. Execute on a LTF Hammer or Bullish Engulfing.
