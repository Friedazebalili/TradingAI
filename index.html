Institutional Order Flow

Liquidity Sweeps Trap

Retail traders place stop-losses above/below obvious equal highs and lows. Institutions drive price through these levels to grab that liquidity to fill their massive orders, before reversing the market.

Fair Value Gaps (FVG) Imbalance

A 3-candle sequence where rapid price delivery creates a gap between the 1st candle's wick and the 3rd candle's wick. The market naturally returns to "fill" this gap to rebalance the order book.

Order Blocks (OB) Sponsor

The last down-candle before a massive institutional up-move (or vice versa). When price returns to this specific block, we expect heavy defense from the institutions that placed the original orders.
